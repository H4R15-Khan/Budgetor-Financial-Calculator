How the 50-30-20 rule works

The 50-30-20 rule is a simple monthly budgeting framework: put about 50% of your after-tax income toward needs, 30% toward wants, and 20% toward savings or investments. Use it as a starting point, then adjust the percentages to match your city, lifestyle, goals, and obligations.
